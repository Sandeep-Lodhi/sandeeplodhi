Call-center business intelligence platform with 45+ self-service reports over telephony and order data, on tables of 50M+ rows.
A chunked, incremental ETL syncs PostgreSQL and SQL Server into a local SQLite read model, taking heavy reports from minutes to milliseconds.
Includes JWT with email-OTP login, per-report access control and guarded Excel/CSV export.
